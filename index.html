Who can use it

Your access depends on the role the Property Custodian gave your account.

Property Unit

Property Custodian

Full access: register items, issue PAR / ICS, transfers, returns, disposals, physical counts and COA submissions.

Inventory Committee

Count teams

Record the physical count for the locations assigned to your team.

Accounting

Head & Staff

Recommend cost and date changes, set useful life and depreciation, and reconcile with the General Ledger.

COA

COA Resident

Read-only: signed documents, released counts, final reconciliations and submitted reports.

How to sign in

  1. Open the system with the button on this page.
  2. Sign in with Google using the email registered for you. CHED staff use their @ched.gov.ph account.
  3. Accounting and COA, first time only: type the one-time code from your invite email, then choose a 6-digit PIN.
  4. Every new session: enter your PIN. Never share your code or PIN.
"Your account is not registered"? You are signed in with an email the system does not know. Switch Google accounts, or ask the Property Custodian to add your email.

Verify a property sticker

Every property sticker has a QR code. Scan it with your phone camera to see the item's details and whether it was verified in the latest physical count. No sign-in needed.

Need help?

For access, PIN resets or questions about your accountability, contact the Property Custodian / Supply Unit, CHED Regional Office VI.
